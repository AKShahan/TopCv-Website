Common Questions

Why is Professional+ a price range?
The final price within LKR 2,500–4,000 depends on the scope of work — specifically whether you need LinkedIn creation from scratch or optimization of an existing profile, and the complexity of your CV. We'll confirm your exact price after a quick consultation.
Are there any hidden fees?
Absolutely not. The price you see is the price you pay. Unlimited revisions are included within your package at no extra cost. We believe in complete transparency.
Do you offer student discounts?
Yes! We offer a special student rate. Contact us with your student ID and we'll apply a discount to your package. We believe every student deserves a strong start — regardless of budget.
How do I pay?
We accept bank transfers and mobile payment methods. A 50% advance is required to begin work, with the remaining 50% due on delivery. We'll share payment details after your consultation.
What if I'm not happy with the result?
Your satisfaction is our priority. We offer unlimited revisions within the scope of your package. If after multiple revision rounds you're genuinely unsatisfied, we'll discuss a resolution that works for both parties — always fairly.

Quick Tips

Career Tips You Can Use Today

01

Quantify Everything

"Managed a team" becomes "Led a 6-person team that delivered 23% revenue growth." Numbers make achievements real.

02

Tailor Every Application

A generic CV gets generic results. Mirror keywords from each job description to pass ATS and show relevance.

03

Optimize Your LinkedIn Headline

Don't just list your job title. Use your headline to communicate your value proposition and target role clearly.

04

The 7-Second Rule

Recruiters spend ~7 seconds on a CV first pass. Put your strongest achievements in the top third of your CV.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to receive my CV?
Standard delivery for the Basic package is 3 business days. Professional+ takes up to 1 week, and the Premium package up to 2 weeks. Rush delivery is available — ask us about it during your consultation.
What information do I need to provide?
Just share your existing CV (if any), your target roles or industries, and a brief description of your career goals. We'll guide you through the rest during our consultation call and may ask follow-up questions as we work.
Can you help fresh graduates with no work experience?
Absolutely — and this is one of our specialties. We know how to present academic achievements, internships, projects, volunteering, and soft skills in a way that genuinely impresses employers looking to hire entry-level talent.
Do you work with clients outside Sri Lanka?
Yes! We work with clients globally. All consultations are conducted online via video call or WhatsApp, so location is never a barrier. We have experience with CVs tailored for UAE, UK, Australia, and other markets.
